01 A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Part creative thinker,
part problem solver.

I earned a Master’s degree in International Business from Aston Business School, where I strengthened my analytical, communication, problem-solving, and client-management skills. My projects explored ESG in fast fashion, Birmingham 150 sustainability, and challenges affecting the LGBTQ+ community through the Birmingham Pride Business Challenge.

On Birmingham 150, I worked with a team to envision an educational bus experience that could engage young people with sustainability. I’m now transitioning into frontend development, building projects with React, Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. I’m excited to combine my business perspective with my growing technical skills to create meaningful, user-friendly web experiences.
